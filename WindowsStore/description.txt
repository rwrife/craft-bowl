CALL THE PLAY. TAKE THE FIELD.

Block Bowl brings fast, arcade-style American football to a colorful block-built stadium. Read the defense, find a gap, and drive downfield in quick 9-on-9 matchups.

YOUR TEAM, YOUR PLAYBOOK
Choose from nine original teams, each with its own colors and roster. Before every snap, pick one of three illustrated plays. Throw to the left, center, or right lane, hand off to a runner, or take off with the quarterback.

MAKE EVERY YARD COUNT
Use turbo to burst through a gap and dive for extra yards. Player health affects performance, while power, speed, endurance, and ability give each roster its own strengths. Balance the big play against the energy you need for the next snap.

FRIDAY-NIGHT ATMOSPHERE
Take the field beneath stadium floodlights, surrounded by a blocky crowd, painted team end zones, and a live scoreboard. Celebrate touchdowns with confetti, fireworks, and team-color lighting.

PLAY YOUR WAY ON WINDOWS
Use a keyboard or a compatible game controller. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, pass with J/K/L, hand off with H, hold Space for turbo, and dive with U. On-screen controls are also available for touch input.

Built for short sessions and football fans who want to call the play and take the field.
